Our capabilities

We build the systems, skills, and strategies growing businesses need to convert ambition into consistent, measurable revenue.

01. Strategy & System

Sales Growth Consulting

Most revenue plateaus are not caused by poor products or weak markets. They are caused by the absence of a structured, repeatable sales system. RevvKraft’s consulting service builds that system from the ground up, diagnosing exact breakdowns and building frameworks that convert effort into consistent revenue.

The 6 Components

  • + Sales Audit: Diagnostic across strategy, pipeline, CRM, and metrics.
  • + Pipeline Structuring: Stage-by-stage design with mandatory deal criteria.
  • + Outbound Playbook: Complete sequences, scripts, and cadences.
  • + Performance Tracking: Metrics framework and revenue forecast model.
  • + Strategy & Market: Positioning analysis and trigger event mapping.
  • + Communication: Optimisation of client-facing sales documents.
Who This Is For: Mid-size companies (10-500 employees) seeking to transition from founder-led sales to a scalable, process-driven organisation.
02. Online Presence

Digital Marketing, Done for You

We manage your complete digital marketing operation. You focus on closing the leads; we handle everything else. We build, run, and optimise every element of your digital marketing programme every month to ensure brand consistency and measurable lead flow.

The 6 Digital Pillars

  • + Pillar 1 - Social Media: We manage your complete social presence, including custom content strategy, creative designs, scheduling, and engagement tactics to build and grow your brand's organic community.
  • + Pillar 2 - LinkedIn Marketing: B2B lead generation campaigns and professional thought leadership positioning designed to connect with decision-makers, build credibility, and fill your sales pipeline.
  • + Pillar 3 - SEO: Increase search visibility through keyword research, search-optimized blog content, link building, and technical optimizations that rank your business higher in organic results.
  • + Pillar 4 - Meta Ads: High-performance advertising campaigns across Facebook and Instagram focusing on paid lead generation, custom audience building, copy testing, and continuous A/B optimization.
  • + Pillar 5 - Google Ads: Optimized Search and Display campaigns that capture high-intent buyers the moment they search for your services. Includes copywriting, bid management, and monthly performance reporting.
  • + Pillar 6 - Google My Business (GMB) Optimisation: Complete local profile optimisation including category setup, photo management, Q&As, and review handling to rank higher in local search results and drive direction requests.
Our Goal: Not simply to increase online activity, but to create a structured digital presence that improves lead quality over time.
03. Physical Impact

Exhibition Stall Design & Mfg.

Your exhibition stall is your brand’s physical ambassador. RevvKraft designs and manufactures exhibition stalls that command attention, communicate your brand’s value proposition clearly, and create the right environment for meaningful business conversations.

What Is Included

  • + Concept & 3D Visualisation: See your stall before fabrication begins.
  • + Structural Engineering: Safe, compliant structures built to venue specs.
  • + Material & Fabrication: High-quality materials for visual impact.
  • + End-to-End Logistics: Includes on-site installation, lighting, and dismantling.
Why It Matters: Every stall is developed with a balance of aesthetics, practicality, and brand communication, ensuring your company stands out.
